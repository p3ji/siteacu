Meet Your Practitioner

Traditional Chinese Medicine & Acupuncture Since 1987 and compassionate patient care.

Linda Xiaochun Leng, a registered Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioner and Acupuncturist, has been practicing traditional Chinese medicine and acupuncture continuously since 1987.

After working extensively in her field, she established her private clinic in 1993. She is in good standing with The College of Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioners and Acupuncturists of Ontario (CTCMPAO) and The Chinese Medicine and Acupuncture Association of Canada (CMAAC).

Linda treats each patient as a complete constitutional individual rather than a collection of isolated symptoms. Patients regularly seek her care for stubborn musculoskeletal pain, chronic gastrointestinal conditions, neurological disorders, autoimmune concerns, women's fertility, and complex chronic cases that have stalled under conventional approaches.

Traditional Acupuncture

Restoring Qi flow and energetic equilibrium
Acupuncture has a documented history of 2,000 to 4,000 years, rooted in classical texts including the Yellow Emperor's Classic of Internal Medicine (Nei Qing Su Wen). Very fine, hair-thin needles stimulate carefully chosen acupoints along the 14 major channels to disperse blockages in Qi flow and restore energetic balance. Regularly paired with infra-red heat lamps and optional electro-acupuncture.

Patient Questions Answered

Clear, honest information regarding acupuncture and herbal medicine.

Unlike hollow hypodermic needles used for blood draws or injections, acupuncture needles are ultra-fine, flexible, and hair-thin. When inserted to the correct depth by a trained practitioner, insertion is virtually painless. As the needle contacts the energetic pathway, patients typically feel a unique mild sensation known in Chinese medicine as Deqi—often described as a slight warm ache, heaviness, or gentle tingling. Most patients enter a deeply restful, nap-like state during their 30-minute session.
At Heritage Acupuncture, patient safety is our paramount priority. We exclusively use pre-sterilized, surgical stainless steel, single-use disposable needles that are unpacked in front of you and safely discarded immediately into biomedical sharps containers. We never reuse needles under any circumstance. Our practice strictly adheres to Ontario CTCMPAO infection prevention and control standards.
While Western pharmaceuticals typically isolate a single synthetic active chemical to suppress a specific symptom, Chinese Herbal Medicine utilizes synergistic botanical combinations. In a classic formula, one or two principal herbs target the root illness, while adjuvant and guiding herbs harmonize digestion, cancel potential side effects, and direct therapeutic action to specific organs. We prepare remedies as easy-to-take soluble granules, powders, or capsules.
Because each patient's constitution and duration of illness is distinct, treatment plans are individualized. Acute conditions (like an acute neck kink or early facial nerve flare) can show major improvement in 3 to 6 visits over two to three weeks. Complex chronic conditions (such as long-standing colitis, neuropathy, or fertility support) generally benefit from weekly sessions over 2 to 3 months alongside tailored botanical formulas. Linda will provide an honest assessment during your initial visit.
